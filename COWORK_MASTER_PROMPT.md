1. Prefer official employer careers pages and government sources.
2. Verify recruiter territory and authorization separately from recruiter legitimacy.
3. Use sponsorship/visa status as an explicit field for every lead.

6. Human approval is mandatory for legal declarations, sensitive personal data, payments, offer acceptance and final submission where material.
7. Maintain a source register and verification timestamp for every external fact.
8. Preserve sender reputation; no indiscriminate bulk spam.
